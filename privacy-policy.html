Privacy, in plain language

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

SevenTask Calculator is designed to do calculations without asking you to create an account. Some information is stored on your device to make the app useful, and a few online features connect to third-party services.

Information stored on your device

Calculator history, preferences, pinned tools, custom calculators and some currency data may be saved in your browser or app storage. This helps the calculator remember your work. This data is not sent to SevenTask Calculator servers by the features described here. You can remove browser or app storage in your device settings; doing so may erase saved data.

Online services

  • Currency conversion: exchange-rate information is requested from the Frankfurter API. The service may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address and request details.
  • Fonts: the website may request fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive standard connection information when fonts are loaded.
  • Website hosting: the website is delivered through Cloudflare infrastructure, which may process technical request data to provide and protect the service.

These providers process information under their own privacy terms. The calculator does not include advertising or analytics trackers in the reviewed web source.

Children and sensitive information

The calculator is a general-purpose tool. Please do not enter sensitive personal information into custom calculator names, formulas or other fields.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can use device settings to clear locally saved data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to certain processing. To ask a privacy question or make a request, contact us using the details below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. We will post the current version on this page.

Privacy contact: kawkabtechlabs@gmail.com
