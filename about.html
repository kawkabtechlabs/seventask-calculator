About SevenTask Calculator

SevenTask Calculator brings everyday maths, finance, science and conversions into one workspace. You can choose a focused calculator, keep recent results in History and return to a calculation without switching between apps.

What you can do

  • Use 136+ calculators and conversion tools in the web workspace.
  • Open a floating calculator and keep another calculation alongside it.
  • Review saved history and use undo and redo while working.
  • Access the available web, Windows and Android versions from the downloads page.

Who makes it

SevenTask Calculator is made by Kawkab Tech Labs. We build practical tools that make numerical work easier to understand. We welcome bug reports and suggestions at kawkabtechlabs@gmail.com.
