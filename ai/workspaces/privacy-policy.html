Privacy, in plain language

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Updated: 23 November 2026 · Web AI release 1.18.55

SevenTask Calculator is designed to do calculations without asking you to create an account. Some information is stored on your device to make the app useful, and a few online features connect to third-party services.

Information stored on your device

Calculator history, theme and other preferences, pinned tools, custom calculators, bubble position and some currency data may be saved in your browser or app storage. This helps the calculator remember your work. The standard calculator and supported offline AI commands perform calculations locally. You can clear browser or app storage in your device settings; doing so may erase saved data.

Settings, notifications and vibration

The Settings menu lets you choose a theme and dropdown size, and control Notifications and Vibration. The web app saves these choices in your browser's local storage. On the web, the Notifications switch currently saves your preference; it does not itself send or stop notifications. In the Android app, notification permission and delivery are controlled by Android settings. The floating calculator may show a service notification while it runs. Vibration gives brief feedback when you tap calculator buttons on supported devices, and can be turned off in Settings. These preferences and button taps are not sent to us.

Web AI assistant, permissions and data

The Web AI assistant requires an internet connection. Typed requests that need assistant help and voice recordings you choose to send are transmitted to SevenTask's hosted service and an external AI service provider for processing. The request may include the text you enter, selected calculator context, relevant calculator names and field labels, or a voice recording. The assistant detects typed and spoken language automatically and replies in that language. Responses return to your current browser session. Chat messages are not intentionally stored by SevenTask; browser history or screenshots you save remain under your control. Cloudflare and Google may process technical connection data under their own terms. With some free service tiers, the AI provider may use submitted prompts and responses to improve its services, as described in its terms. Do not send passwords, payment details, health data, or other sensitive information. The external AI service provider’s own terms and privacy policy also apply.

On the website, the AI microphone starts only after you tap it and grant browser permission. Voice audio sent through the website is processed by SevenTask’s hosted service and an external AI provider; it is not added to calculator history. In the Android app, tapping the AI microphone requests Android microphone permission and opens the device speech-recognition service (Google Speech Services when available). That service processes the audio under its own terms and returns recognized text to SevenTask. The Android app sends the recognized text and relevant calculator context to SevenTask’s hosted AI service for a reply; raw microphone audio is not sent to SevenTask’s AI endpoint by this native voice flow. Internet access is needed for AI replies. You can cancel speech recognition before submitting. Closing the AI screen ends the interaction. Attachments and drawings in the web preview are not sent to the assistant.

SevenTask history and preferences are saved in this browser when enabled. You can clear them from the calculator or your browser settings. The Web AI chat itself is temporary and is not added to calculator history unless you explicitly save a calculation there.

Local calculations, caching and service protection

Supported arithmetic and calculator commands run on your device without sending that request to the AI provider. Requests requiring an AI explanation or interpretation, and voice clips you submit, are sent for online processing. The service worker stores application resources for faster loading and offline calculator access; API requests bypass that cache. Abuse protection uses the connecting IP address and short-lived request counters. Infrastructure providers may also retain technical logs according to their own policies; SevenTask does not promise that provider logs contain no request information.

Online services

The website loads Google Fonts and Google AdSense and is hosted through Cloudflare. These services may receive technical connection information and use cookies or other storage under their own policies. Currency conversion may contact the Frankfurter API. Cloudflare Web Analytics may also collect website performance and usage information. Advertising consent availability depends on region and configured consent controls; this policy does not itself grant advertising consent. Manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center.

Feedback, sharing and updates

If you choose Send Feedback, your email app opens with our address. Any message and personal details you send are handled by your email provider and received by us. Sharing the app opens your device's share options; the service you choose handles what you share. Download Latest Version checks release information on our website and can display the available version and APK size. When Auto-Update is on, the Android app checks for a newer version when it opens; you can turn this off in Settings. That request may send standard connection information such as your IP address. The APK downloads only if you choose the download or update action, and Android asks before installation.

Floating calculator

On supported Android versions, the floating calculator may request permission to display over other apps. You can manage this permission in Android settings. The permission enables the floating window; it does not by itself send your calculations to us.

Children and sensitive information

The calculator is a general-purpose tool. Please do not enter sensitive personal information into custom calculator names, formulas or other fields.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can use device settings to clear locally saved data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to certain processing. To ask a privacy question or make a request, contact us using the details below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. We will post the current version on this page.
